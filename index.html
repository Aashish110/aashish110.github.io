Selected work

Robotics and UAV projects

DRDO Project

Collaborative Payload Transport Using Four Quadcopters

Developed a PX4-ROS2 leader-follower UAV swarm using RTK and MQTT communication, achieving outdoor collaborative transport of a 1 kg payload.

MEITy

Passenger Drone - 16 Rotor System

Designed and developed a passenger-class drone platform, leading structure, electronics, and control system design for a 75 kg payload.

Healthcare Robotics

Tele-Echography Robotic Platform

Built a robotic healthcare platform using NEMA motors, encoders, Dynamixel steppers, Raspberry Pi, and custom mechanical integration.

Prototypes

Robotic Arms, Prosthetic Hands, CNC and IoT Systems

Designed 6-DOF robotic arms, five prosthetic hand prototypes, 3D printer systems, CNC drill and mill machines, wire-bending machines, automation devices, and smart IoT prototypes.
